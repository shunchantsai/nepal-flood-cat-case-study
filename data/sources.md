# Sources

## Hazard / event science
- USGS — provisional event seismic/geological analysis and official disaster maps
- ICIMOD — hydrological monitoring data, cross-border disaster investigation direction
- Nepal NDRRMA / Nepal Police — official casualty and missing-persons statistics
- Reuters / AP — field reporting, rescue progress
- Shugar et al. (2021), *Science* — "A massive rock and ice avalanche caused the 2021 disaster at Chamoli, Indian Himalaya" (DOI: 10.1126/science.abh4455) — mechanism precedent for seismic-signal misattribution
- Peng et al. (2022), *Journal of Hydrology* — ice-debris dam breach dynamics (DOI: 10.1016/j.jhydrol.2021.127252)
- Khadka et al. (2024) — cross-border (China-Nepal) GLOF exposure modelling
- Gouli et al. (2025) — extreme-scenario peak discharge modelling for this basin
- Kirschbaum et al. (2019), *Frontiers in Earth Science* — cascading mountain hazard chain framework (Fig. 2D)
